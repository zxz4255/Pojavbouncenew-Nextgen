# 089 reference/provenance

The six reference JPEGs were supplied by the user. Their filenames and SHA256 hashes, cropped-coordinate anchors and selected feature traces are recorded in `reference-spec.json`. The date-like strings in the filenames identify supplied files; they are not an asserted build date or independently authenticated capture date.

The visible original client appears to carry Pharos branding, but no original rendering shader/source or license for that client was obtained. This work is an analytic, reference-traced material approximation, NOT a port of that client's code, NOT the screenshot creator's original shader and NOT a verified pixel-identical reproduction. Traced feature points are partly obscured by foreground text. Unmeasured portions, widths, intensities and color/transmission are approximate. JPEG scaling/compression and underlying scenery are confounds.

The optional review HTML embeds limited crops of the user-provided images strictly to compare their supplied reference with labelled offscreen samples. No reference image, username, skin, item graphic, text or scenery is embedded into runtime shader resources. The shader uses the live framebuffer and analytic reflection functions.

The existing MIT-derived edge refraction and shared optical transport from086 remain credited by `../glass086/`, including the LiquidGlassShader source/license and pinned upstream IDs. The new analytic tracing/routing changes are under the containing project's GPL-3.0-or-later license. No newly discovered original-client source is claimed.

The reflection traces were used in fitting AND in the local feature-position test. That test is not an independent benchmark. A small coordinate error does not establish matching reflectance, blur, layout, text, animation or full-image pixels.
